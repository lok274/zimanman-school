# 字慢慢詞語圖片

2026-10-22 使用內置 imagegen 工具，以文字生成模式分別製作六張 AI 寫實圖片。它們並非實地拍攝照片。人物及手部均為虛構。

圖片使用白色無縫背景，保留清楚的物件輪廓，沒有文字、品牌或環境雜物。形容詞以同類物件作比較，方便孩子理解意思。圖片只作詞義提示，不能代替老師解釋或判斷學習成效。

## 生成提示詞

每次生成使用以下共同提示詞，再接上對應詞語的提示詞。共六次獨立生成。

```text
Create one square 1024x1024 classroom vocabulary image. Medium: highly photorealistic studio product photography, physically convincing real materials and proportions, not an illustration, not a cartoon, not a 3D render. Background must be a completely uniform pure white RGB #FFFFFF seamless field, including all four corners and all outer margins. No gray background, no cast shadows outside the subjects, no gradients, no floor line, no vignette, no borders, no words, no Chinese characters, no letters, no numerals, no logo, no watermark. Center the complete subjects with a generous 10 percent white safety margin. Keep composition simple and readable in a small elementary-school tablet image.
```

```text
Scientific-educational image for the Chinese concept 粗 (thick). Show exactly two short pieces of the SAME natural tan braided rope, parallel horizontal rows and equal length. Upper rope very thick (about four times the lower rope diameter), lower rope thin. Same beige fiber and braid texture, clear slightly frayed cut ends, gentle natural curves, no knots or coils. The two actual ropes are isolated on white. Make the thickness comparison unmistakable, upper thick rope is the main subject.
```
